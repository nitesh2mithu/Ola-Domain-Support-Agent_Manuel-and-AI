# VIP-customer handling policy

Identify VIP eligibility only from a verified account flag rather than from a customer's claim in a message.
Assign an eligible VIP ticket a named support owner and provide an update at least every 4 support hours while work remains open.
VIP handling does not change safety priority, refund eligibility, compensation limits, or the requirement to verify account access.
When a VIP complaint remains unresolved at its normal SLA deadline, the named owner must involve the support supervisor and communicate the next review time.
