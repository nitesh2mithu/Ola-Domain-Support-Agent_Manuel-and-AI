# Business-hours and holiday-support policy

Standard support hours are 09:00 to 18:00 Asia/Kolkata from Monday through Saturday, excluding holidays listed in the team's published support calendar.
Support-hour SLA clocks pause outside those hours and resume at the next opening, while business-day refund processing uses Monday through Friday excluding listed holidays.
P1 safety and major-outage reports reach the on-call team at all times, including Sundays and holidays, and use elapsed-time SLA clocks.
For other requests received outside standard hours, acknowledge receipt automatically and state when staffed support resumes without promising immediate resolution.
