# SLA-by-severity policy

For P1 severity, acknowledge within 15 elapsed minutes and target resolution or a documented workaround within 4 elapsed hours.
For P2 severity, acknowledge within 1 support hour and target resolution within 24 support hours.
For P3 severity, acknowledge within 4 support hours and target resolution within 72 support hours; for P4 severity, acknowledge within 8 support hours and target resolution within 120 support hours.
These SLA targets use the business-hours policy for support-hour calculations and require an update before a deadline is missed, while external dependencies must be documented rather than presented as guaranteed completion times.
