# Repeat-complaint handling policy

Treat a report as a repeat complaint when the same customer reports the same underlying issue within 30 days of the earlier ticket.
Link the new report to the earlier ticket and reopen that ticket when the previous resolution has not held, preserving the original history.
Assign a support supervisor to review the root cause on the second repeat, and raise priority when the facts show greater impact rather than because of complaint count alone.
Tell the customer which owner is investigating and avoid asking for evidence already present in the linked history.
