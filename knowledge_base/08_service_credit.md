# Service-credit policy

A support supervisor may approve a noncash service credit up to INR 150 for a verified service interruption when a cash refund does not already compensate the same loss.
Apply an approved service credit to the customer's fabricated capstone account balance, with validity of 30 days from issuance.
Service credits cannot be transferred or exchanged for cash, and a credit above INR 150 requires the operations manager's approval.
Record the reason, approval, amount and expiry in the ticket and communicate those terms before issuing the credit.
