# Data-retention policy for tickets

Retain a support ticket and its attached communication for 180 days after the ticket is closed, then delete identifying details through the scheduled retention process.
For a resolved ticket that remains open, the support owner must either close it after confirming resolution or document why further work is required rather than leave it indefinitely unresolved.
Restrict ticket access to staff assigned to support duties, and use masked phone numbers in diagnostic logs.
An authorized legal hold pauses deletion only for the affected records, must be documented and reviewed every 30 days, and allows deletion to resume after the hold is released.
