# Customer communication channel policy

Use in-app support as the primary communication channel for rider and driver ticket updates, with email as the alternative when account access is unavailable.
A support agent may offer a callback for an urgent issue only after the customer requests or consents to the call.
Before sharing ticket details on an alternative channel, complete the approved account-verification process and avoid exposing another customer's information.
Never request an OTP, full payment-card details, or an account password, and keep every customer update attached to the ticket record.
