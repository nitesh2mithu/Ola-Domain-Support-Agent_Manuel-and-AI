# Escalation matrix

Route billing and refund disputes to the billing specialist, technical issues and product defects to the technical specialist, and account-access failures to the account-security specialist.
Escalate a P1 safety report immediately to the duty incident lead, who coordinates the responsible specialist and emergency guidance when appropriate.
Escalate a P2 ticket to the support supervisor if its acknowledgement deadline is missed, and escalate any unresolved ticket before its resolution target expires.
The receiving owner must accept the handoff, and the original owner remains responsible for customer updates until acceptance is recorded.
