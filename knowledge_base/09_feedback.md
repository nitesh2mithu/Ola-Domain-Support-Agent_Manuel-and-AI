# Feedback-collection process

After a ticket is resolved, offer one optional in-app feedback survey asking for a satisfaction rating from 1 to 5 and a short comment.
If the customer does not respond, send at most one reminder after 48 hours and do not make feedback a condition of receiving support.
A rating of 1 or 2 or a comment indicating an unresolved issue triggers a supervisor review of the resolution.
Store feedback against the ticket using the ticket-retention policy, and report aggregated themes without identifying individual customers.
