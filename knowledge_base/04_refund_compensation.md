# Refund and compensation policy

A billing specialist may approve a refund after verifying a duplicate charge, a confirmed overcharge, or a charge for a service that was not delivered.
Refund only the verified incorrect amount to the original payment method, with a target processing time of 5 to 7 business days after approval.
A support supervisor may approve goodwill compensation up to INR 200 for a documented service failure, while larger compensation requires the operations manager's approval.
Do not promise a refund or compensation before approval, and record whether a service credit already covers the same loss to prevent duplicate reimbursement.
