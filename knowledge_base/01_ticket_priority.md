# Ticket-priority classification rules

Assign P1 priority when a report describes an immediate safety concern or a widespread inability to use the ride service.
Assign P2 priority to a blocked account or an incorrect charge that prevents a rider or driver from continuing normal service use.
Assign P3 priority to a nonblocking technical issue or product defect, and assign P4 priority to a general inquiry or feedback request.
When several conditions apply, record the highest priority and the facts that justify it without treating VIP status as a safety severity.
