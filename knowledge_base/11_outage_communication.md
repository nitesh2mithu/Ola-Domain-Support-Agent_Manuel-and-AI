# Outage-communication protocol

When monitoring or multiple validated tickets confirm an outage, the duty incident lead opens an incident record and identifies the affected service and regions.
Publish an initial in-app notice within 15 elapsed minutes of incident confirmation and update it every 30 elapsed minutes while disruption continues.
Each notice must distinguish confirmed impact from investigation findings and provide only verified workarounds, with no unconfirmed restoration promise.
After recovery is verified, publish a recovery notice and attach a factual incident summary to related support tickets within 2 business days.
